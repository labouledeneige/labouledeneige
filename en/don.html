Where your donation goes

Closely tracked use of funds

In 2025, the association received CHF 87'908 in donations and redistributed CHF 84'716 in projects: agricultural support, teacher salaries at the Kompienga secondary school, schooling for children, the Ouagadougou primary school, the sewing school. Operating costs (bank, postal account) represent 0.36% of expenses: nearly all of your donation goes directly to the field.
